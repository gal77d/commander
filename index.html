How to place the phone
  • Phone on the floor in front of you, standing up (leaning on something), about 1 m in front of your head. Look straight at the camera.
  • It must see your legs behind you (between your arms) – that's how it knows if you're on your knees. Knee push-ups don't count.
  • Your shoulders, elbows and hands must stay in the picture the whole time.
  • At the start, hold the top position with straight arms for a second – it learns your height.
